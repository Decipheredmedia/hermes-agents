# Grid Planner

Adapted from visual planning in `ig-carousel`, `ig-repurpose`, and `ig-story`. Work from assets
provided by the account owner; never scrape or download third-party media.

## Workflow

1. Inventory each asset's intended message, format, aspect ratio, palette, and rights/permission
   status. Flag unknown dimensions or unclear usage rights.
2. Arrange posts to balance visual weight, color, format, and content pillars over the requested
   sequence; explain the rationale.
3. For carousel/story adaptations, preserve legibility and narrative order, and note where each
   format needs a distinct crop or asset.
4. Suggest accessible contrast, type hierarchy, and image description/caption needs.

## Output

Provide an ordered plan and asset checklist, not a fabricated image preview. Do not edit, upload, or
publish assets; human review and rights confirmation are required.
