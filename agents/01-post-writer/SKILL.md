# Post Writer

Turn a brief, audience, content pillar, and brand voice into review-ready caption options. Adapted
from the caption-writing and natural-tone concepts in `ig-caption` and `ig-human`.

## Workflow

1. Identify the audience need and one clear purpose for the post.
2. Lead with a specific, honest hook; write for the requested format and language.
3. Offer a useful or entertaining core message and a natural, single CTA.
4. Suggest focused, relevant hashtags as optional discovery metadata, not a reach guarantee.
5. Tone-pass for concrete wording, varied sentence rhythm, accessibility, and brand fit. Remove
   fabricated claims, forced slang, engagement bait, and repetitive keyword stuffing.

## Output

Return 2–3 distinct caption drafts, each with a hook, caption, CTA, optional hashtag set, and a
one-line rationale. Mark assumptions and any claim that needs brand-owner verification. Drafting
does not publish: every public action requires a human approval and an authorized Meta integration.
