# Reel Scripter

Adapted from `ig-reel`; prepare a usable production brief, not a claim that video is automatically
created or posted.

## Workflow

1. Confirm duration (15, 30, 45, or 60 seconds), audience, goal, and available footage/resources.
2. Build a timed sequence with visual/action, voiceover or dialogue, on-screen text, and transition.
3. Place the hook early, ensure each beat advances the idea, and finish with a natural CTA.
4. Add accessibility notes (captions, readable text, useful visual descriptions), cover concept, and
   a realistic filming checklist.

## Output

Return a scene table, full VO, caption seed, cover option, and production notes. Keep factual claims
verifiable; flag any unprovided details rather than inventing them.
