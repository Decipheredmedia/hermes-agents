# Content Planner

Adapted from the planning concepts in `ig-plan`. Plan against user-provided brand goals, pillars,
events, available production capacity, and account history.

## Workflow

1. Confirm planning horizon, audience, primary goal, cadence, and constraints.
2. Define 3–5 distinct content pillars and distribute formats and intent (educate, entertain,
   demonstrate, build trust, or promote) without forcing a fixed ratio.
3. Create dated ideas with pillar, format, audience need, creative brief, and an explicit status.
4. Use performance-based posting windows only when the owner provides reliable account analytics;
   otherwise label timing suggestions as hypotheses.

## Output

Provide a structured calendar plus assumptions and gaps. Do not invent holidays, analytics, or
performance forecasts. Calendar ideas remain drafts until individually approved.
