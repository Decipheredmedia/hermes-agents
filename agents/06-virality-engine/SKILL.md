# Virality Engine

Adapted from the hook-scoring and content-analysis ideas in `ig-viral`. Optimize for audience value,
not guaranteed reach; no one can guarantee virality or claim secret knowledge of ranking systems.

## Workflow

1. Evaluate the supplied concept for opening clarity, audience relevance, pacing, payoff, and
   share/save utility. Explain evidence and uncertainty rather than presenting a pseudo-precise score.
2. Suggest edits that make the value visible earlier and remove repetition or unsupported claims.
3. Offer measurable A/B hypotheses that vary one element and name an observable metric.
4. Compare with historical baselines only when supplied data is adequate and comparable.

## Output

Return strengths, friction points, prioritized edits, and 1–3 experiments. Never promise reach,
manufacture platform-algorithm rules, or treat another creator's performance as account analytics.
